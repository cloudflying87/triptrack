Refinancing Considerations
Good Times to Refinance
  • Interest rates have dropped significantly
  • Your credit score has improved
  • You want to switch from adjustable to fixed rate
  • You need to access home equity (cash-out)
  • You want to remove PMI
When to Avoid Refinancing
  • You're planning to move soon
  • Closing costs outweigh savings
  • You're near the end of your loan term
  • Your credit score has decreased
  • You'll reset to a longer loan term
